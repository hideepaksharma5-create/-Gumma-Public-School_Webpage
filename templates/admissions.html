Document Checklist

Required Documents for Admission

Ensure you have authentic self-attested copies of the following documents ready for submission and verification.

1

Birth Certificate

Original & self-attested copy issued by Municipal Corporation, Registrar of Births, or Gram Panchayat (mandatory for Pre-Primary to Grade I).

2

Transfer Certificate (TC)

Original TC from previous recognized school, counter-signed by competent educational authority (mandatory for Grades II through XII admissions).

3

Report Cards / Marksheets

Photocopy of previous class final annual progress report or Class X Board Marksheet for Grade XI Science/Commerce streams.

4

Aadhaar & Residence Proof

Copy of Student's Aadhaar card and Father/Mother/Guardian Aadhaar card or Bonafide Himachali Certificate.

5

Recent Color Photographs

4 passport-size photographs of the student in white background, plus 2 passport-size photographs of each parent/guardian.

6

Medical Fitness & Category

Certified Blood Group record, immunization certificate, and SC/ST/OBC/EWS certificate if availing fee concession.

Upload Note: Digital uploads on the Online Portal must be in PDF or JPG format (maximum 2MB per file). Original documents must be produced for physical verification during seat allotment.
