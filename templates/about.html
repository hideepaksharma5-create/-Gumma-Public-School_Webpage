Our Guiding North Star

Vision & Mission

Anchored in core human values while embracing modern pedagogy to shape globally competent citizens.

Our Vision

To be a benchmark of inclusive educational distinction in Eastern India, empowering students from all walks of life with critical thinking, ethical resilience, and global leadership skills.

Our Mission

To deliver learner-centric education through experiential STEM methods, vibrant arts and athletic development, and a nurturing environment that instills respect, empathy, and national pride.

Core Values

Knowledge: Lifelong thirst for learning.
Integrity: Unwavering honesty and accountability.
Inclusion: Celebrating diversity and equal opportunity.

Leadership Message

From the Principal's Desk

"Dear Parents, Students, and Well-Wishers,

It is my absolute privilege to welcome you to Gumma Public School. Since our humble beginnings, our single-minded pursuit has been to bridge the gap between quality institutional education and regional accessibility.

We believe that education must go beyond textbooks and examinations. Our pedagogical framework fosters intellectual rigor, creative problem solving, and empathetic citizenship. Our Milestones

The Journey of Gumma Public School

Tracing our growth from a visionary school with 50 students to a premier 15-acre HP Board institution.

1998

Founding Year

Established with a vision to bring standardized English-medium education to students in Gumma and surrounding Shimla towns.

2008

HP Board Affiliation

Awarded full affiliation by the Himachal Pradesh Board of School Education with top-tier science and computer laboratories.

2020 - Present

Digital & STEM Renaissance

Expanded to 100% smart classrooms, robotics and AI workshops, an athletic complex, and GPS-tracked transport bus network.
