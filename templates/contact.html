Campus Directions & Location

Located conveniently near Water Pumping Station, Gumma, Shimla District, Himachal Pradesh - 171018.

🚆 By Train

Nearest railway connectivity is Shimla Railway Station (SML) on the UNESCO World Heritage Kalka-Shimla mountain rail link (~42 km), with broad-gauge express connectivity via Kalka Junction (KLK) and Chandigarh.

🚌 By Road & Bus

Regular Himachal Road Transport Corporation (HRTC) buses and private coaches operate on NH-5 connecting Gumma with Shimla ISBT, Theog, Narkanda, and Rampur.

🚍 School Bus Fleet

Our GPS-monitored yellow school buses service over 25 designated pickup nodes across Gumma, Theog corridor, and adjacent Shimla rural routes.
