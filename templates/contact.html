Campus Directions & Location

Located strategically on the Water pumping station in Gumma ,Shimla District.

🚆 By Train

Nearest Railway Station is Paralakhemundi (PLH) ~28 km, and Berhampur (BAM) ~115 km on the main Howrah-Chennai corridor.

🚌 By Road & Bus

Direct OSRTC and private bus connectivity from Paralakhemundi, Mohana, Gunupur, and Rayagada stopping at Gumma Town Centre.

🚍 School Bus Fleet

Our GPS-monitored yellow school buses service over 25 designated pickup nodes across Gumma, Rayagada road, and nearby towns.
