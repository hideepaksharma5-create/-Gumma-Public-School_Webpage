Academic Structure

Educational Pathways

Aligned with CBSE guidelines and the National Education Policy (NEP) for holistic learning.

Early Years Foundation

Pre-Primary (Nursery, LKG, UKG)

Play-way methodology focusing on motor skills, phonics, number sense, storytelling, music, and social adaptation in a vibrant child-centric wing.

  • Sensory & Activity Kits
  • Phonics & Language Immersion
  • Safe Indoor Play Zone
Preparatory Stage

Primary School (Grades I – V)

Building strong foundations in English, Regional Language, Mathematics, Environmental Science, Computer Fundamentals, and Creative Arts.

  • Interactive Smartboards
  • Math Lab & Reading Circles
  • Weekly Sports & Yoga
Middle Stage

Middle School (Grades VI – VIII)

Fostering analytical curiosity with specialized subjects in General Science (Physics, Chem, Bio), Social Sciences, Coding Basics, and Third Language.

  • Hands-on Science Labs
  • Robotics & Scratch Programming
  • Debating & Quiz Club
Secondary Stage

Secondary (Grades IX & X)

Comprehensive preparation for the CBSE All India Secondary School Examination (AISSE) with regular mock tests and remedial mentorship.

  • HP Board Alignment
  • Career Counseling Workshops
  • Pre-board Assessment Cycles
Senior Secondary

Science Stream (XI & XII)

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, and Computer Science (Python), coupled with integrated coaching for JEE, NEET, and Olympiads.

  • Advanced Research Labs
  • Olympiad Problem Solving
  • Expert Guest Seminars
Senior Secondary

Commerce Stream (XI & XII)

Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Mathematics/Informatics Practices, developing financial literacy and entrepreneurship aptitude.

  • Financial Modeling Projects
  • Commerce & Stock Club
  • CA Foundation Mentorship

Guidelines & Code

Academic Rules & Discipline

Core principles governing attendance, assessments, and campus conduct.

As per HP Board regulations, students must maintain at least 75% attendance throughout the academic session to be eligible for Terminal and Board examinations. Medical leaves require authorized doctor certificates submitted within 3 days.
The academic year is divided into two terms with 3 Periodic Tests (PT1, PT2, PT3), subject enrichment activities, portfolio notebooks, and Term-End Examinations. Report cards are released in Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTM).
Students must wear prescribed lab coats in science laboratories. Mobile phones and unauthorized electronic devices are strictly prohibited during school hours. Strict zero-tolerance policy against bullying or damage to property.
