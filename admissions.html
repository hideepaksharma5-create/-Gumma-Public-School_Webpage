Required Documents for Admission

Please ensure original and self-attested photocopies are kept ready.

✓
Birth Certificate

Issued by Municipal Corporation or Gram Panchayat (Mandatory for Pre-Primary & Grade I).

✓
Transfer Certificate (TC)

Countersigned by District Education Officer (DEO/CBSE) for Grade II and above.

✓
Past Academic Report Card

Original progress card or provisional mark sheet of previous academic year.

✓
Aadhaar Card Copies

Photocopy of student's Aadhaar card and parents'/guardians' Aadhaar cards.

✓
Passport Size Photographs

6 recent passport-size photos of the child and 2 photos of both parents.

✓
Category / Medical Certificate

SC/ST/OBC certificate or blood group / vaccination record if applicable.
